## Visual aesthetic notes (for hyperframes recreation)

- **Layout**: kicker/category label (`사회 · 교육` or `[단독]`) → bold
  multi-line headline → thin gray rule → gray byline (`○○일보 · 입력
  2025.11.24`). A small accent pill replaces the classic red `[단독]` tag.
- **Type**: Pretendard 800-900 reads perfectly as Korean news headline.
  Tight line-height ~1.15, -2% to -3% letter-spacing, big size contrast
  vs kicker and byline.
- **Color**: two options —
  (a) off-white "paper" card (#F4F1EA-ish, faint grain, subtle drop
      shadow, slight torn edge) floating on the navy bg, OR
  (b) near-white headline on navy with a thin cobalt rule + desaturated
      gray byline. Keep ONE red cue (small category dot or underline) for
      "news" recognition even in dark mode.
- **Montage motion**: 3-4 headline cards stack with quick scale-in +
  slight rotation (±1.5°), a "stamp" SFX, then a red `F` / `징계` stamp
  slamming over each — that's the "ban-and-punish old way" beat.
